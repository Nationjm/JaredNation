Project Overview

Fall Freely: A Fall Detection and Safety System

Fall Freely is an innovative wearable and audio-responsive interface designed to assist individuals with fall risk, ADHD, or other conditions that benefit from intelligent safety monitoring and task awareness. Our goal was to enhance user independence through non-intrusive, sound-based cues and contextual alerts.

Key Features Implemented:

  • Sound-based alerts designed to be non-disruptive and emotionally attuned to user preferences
  • Sensor integration (e.g., accelerometer) to detect falls and sudden motion
  • Biometric and environmental awareness for adjusting prompts based on stress or context
  • Task prioritization system that promotes action without relying on annoying alarms
  • Calendar and reminder integration for contextual, just-in-time support

Tools Used:

Figma, Arduino (or eSense), HTML, CSS, JavaScript, User Testing, Paper Prototyping

Project Evolution

The development of Fall Freely was informed by user research and multiple iterations. Here's how it evolved:

Step 1: Research & Personas

We conducted interviews and created personas representing individuals with ADHD and fall risk. Our affinity diagram helped us identify pain points like overwhelming alarms and lack of contextual support.

Step 2: Low Fidelity Prototype

Sketches and low-fidelity wireframes helped us conceptualize an interface focused on calm, sound-based interaction instead of visual or vibrating alerts.

Step 3: Mid Fidelity Prototype

We created interactive prototypes to test how users respond to different types of sound prompts, adjusting tone, frequency, and emotional tone.
